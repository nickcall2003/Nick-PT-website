About the coaching

Answer the call

I've lived this, not just studied it. Here's how I coach and what you get when you train with me.

I was the skinny kid. Out of high school I weighed 100 pounds — and in about eight months of real work I built myself up to a strong, healthy 150. I've trained consistently ever since to keep it. Everything I coach, I've actually lived — I know what it's like to look in the mirror and want something different, and what changes when you finally have a plan and someone holding you to it.

But this isn't only about my story. I've helped plenty of people go the other direction too — dropping weight, getting leaner, and finally feeling in control of their training. Whether you want to build muscle or lose fat, I've been on both sides of that fight and I know how to get you there.

I'm also finishing my Bachelor's degree in Physical Therapy at the University of Memphis (December 2026), so your coaching is grounded in how the body actually moves and recovers — not guesswork. And every plan I write is built from scratch — no two clients get the same workouts or nutrition. Your body, your goals, your schedule, your plan.

My job is to make progress simple: a clear plan for training, cardio, mobility and food, honest coaching on your form, and someone holding you to the standard you set for yourself. Motivation comes and goes — structure and accountability carry you on the days it's missing. That's what I'm here for.
