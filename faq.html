Good to know

Questions

The stuff people usually ask before their first session. If yours isn't here, just reach out.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first 30-minute session is completely free, with no cost, no obligation and no awkward sales pitch. It's a chance to meet, move, and see how I coach before you commit to anything.

How do I claim my free session?

Text, email or fill out the form on the contact page. Tell me your goals and when you're free, and I'll get you scheduled.

Do I need any experience to start?

Not at all. Every plan is built around where you are right now, whether it's your first session ever or your five hundredth.

Do you build a custom plan, or is it the same for everyone?

Everything is built from scratch. Your plan is shaped around your body, your goals, your schedule, and what you actually enjoy doing.

Can you help me lose weight, not just build muscle?

Absolutely. I built myself from 100 to 150 pounds, and I've also helped plenty of people lose weight and get leaner. Whichever direction you're headed, I've coached it.

How long are sessions?

Monthly and Weekly sessions are a full 60 minutes. Express sessions are a focused 30 minutes for tighter schedules. The free intro session is also 30 minutes.

I've never trained before, or I'm really out of shape. Is that okay?

That's exactly who this is for. Everyone starts somewhere, and your plan meets you where you are today. No judgment, ever.

Is training in person or online?

Both. In-person coaching is one-on-one at Crunch Fitness in Cordova, where I can coach your form in real time. Online coaching works from anywhere with a custom plan, weekly check-ins and form reviews.

How does online coaching work?

I build your plan in your private app, you train when it fits and log your sets, and you send a weekly check-in. I review your week, reply with next steps, and adjust your plan. You can also text me videos for form checks. See the full breakdown.

Where do in-person sessions take place?

At Crunch Fitness on Germantown Parkway in Cordova. Reach out and we'll set up your times.

Do I need my own gym membership?

For in-person training, yes. You'll need your own membership at Crunch Fitness Cordova. Online clients can train at the gym of their choice.

What's included with a program?

Every program includes a personalized strength plan, a cardio plan, mobility and flexibility guidance, a 7-day nutrition plan, your private client app, and direct access to me.

What is the client app?

A private login where you can see your workouts, log your sets, check off meals, track your weight, and see your sessions or send your check-in. Only you and I can see your folder.

Do you help with nutrition?

Yes. Every program includes a 7-day nutrition plan built around you, so your training and your eating pull in the same direction from day one.

How soon will I see results?

With consistent training and nutrition, most people feel stronger and more energized within a few weeks, and see physical changes over the first couple of months. Results vary, and the people who follow the plan and keep showing up do best.

What should I bring?

Water, clothes you can move in, and shoes with decent support.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Cash App or Apple Cash. Payment is due before your first session or training week of the period, based on your plan, and the app reminds you when it's coming up.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel?

No long-term contract. It's month to month, so you stay because it's working.

What if I need to reschedule?

Give me at least 24 hours notice and we'll move it. Your in-person training times stay reserved for you each week.

Can I switch programs?

Yes. Life changes and your plan can change with it. Just let me know and we'll adjust.

Do you train women?

Absolutely. I coach men and women at every experience level, and every plan is built around you as an individual.

How do I get started?

Claim your free 30-minute session. Text or email me, and if it's a fit I'll build your plan and get you started.
